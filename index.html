Promotion Rules
Referral Program
• Share your personal referral link or invitation code with friends.
• When someone registers using your code, you instantly earn a $5 sign-up bonus.
• Once your referral subscribes to an investment tier (M1–M6), you automatically earn a one-time team commission based on their subscription amount, across 3 generations: 12% Gen 1, 5% Gen 2, 2% Gen 3.
• Team commissions are credited to your withdrawable balance in real time, every day.
• There is no limit to how many people you can invite or how much you can earn.
Account Levels (M1–M6)
Each tier locks a fixed principal amount and pays a fixed daily rate of return. Higher tiers unlock a higher daily rate and more daily tasks. See the Account Level page for full details on each tier.
